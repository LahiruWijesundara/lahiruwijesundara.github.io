The Edge of The World is an open-world action RPG set in a richly detailed fantasy universe. Players explore vast landscapes, engage in combat, and complete quests to uncover the mysteries of the edge of the world. The project focuses on creating an immersive gameplay experience with dynamic combat and intelligent AI.

      <p class="text-justify">Here is a detailed breakdown of my contribution to the project:</p>
      <ul class="mb-0">
        <!-- Point 1 -->
        <li><strong>Player Combat System:</strong>
          <ul>
            <li>Implemented a comprehensive player combat system, including weapon motion-based hit detection for realistic and responsive combat.</li>
            <li>Developed various attack types and combos, providing players with a range of combat options.</li>         
          </ul>
        </li>
        <!-- Point 2 -->
        <li><strong>Enemy AI:</strong>
          <ul>
            <li>Designed and implemented advanced enemy AI using Unreal Engine’s behaviour tree, environment query system (EQS), and AI perception system.</li>
            <li>Created diverse enemy behaviours and tactics, enhancing the challenge and engagement of combat encounters.</li>         
          </ul>
        </li>
        <!-- Point 3 -->
        <li><strong>Team Collaboration:</strong>
          <ul>
            <li>Collaborated closely with designers, artists, and other programmers to align gameplay mechanics with the overall vision of the game.</li>
            <li>Participated in regular team meetings to discuss progress, share feedback, and address challenges.</li>         
          </ul>
        </li>
        <!-- Point 4 -->
        <li><strong>Challenges and Solutions:</strong>
          <ul>
            <li>Challenge: Creating a dynamic and responsive combat system.</li>
            <li>Solution: Implemented weapon motion-based hit detection and refined combat mechanics through iterative testing and player feedback.</li>  
            <li>Challenge: Developing intelligent and varied enemy AI.</li>
            <li>Solution: Utilized Unreal Engine’s advanced AI tools to create diverse enemy behaviours, enhancing the combat experience.</li>  
            <li>Challenge: Ensuring a seamless open-world experience.</li>
            <li>Solution: Optimized game assets and code and implemented dynamic weather and day-night cycles to create a living, breathing game world.</li>  
          </ul>
        </li>
      </ul>
